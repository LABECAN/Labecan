Welcome to Labecan


The Laboratory of Bioecology and Conservation of Neotropical Birds - LABECAN is part of the Ecology and Conservation Sector of the Institute of Biological and Health Sciences - ICBS at the Federal University of Alagoas - UFAL.
It was established in 2010 under the coordination of biologist and professor Dr. Márcio Efe, where research is conducted on marine and forest birds in zoology and ecology topics, primarily focused on building knowledge about natural history, reproduction, and conservation.
It develops its projects in partnership with national and international researchers on Brazilian islands with marine birds and in the Pernambuco Endemism Center with forest birds, prioritizing endangered bird species.
